# Proof release: six vertices, four colors

## Theorem

Over arbitrary complex bicolored edge weights, the six-vertex four-color equation system has no solution:

\[
\neg\exists W:\operatorname{WeightsN}(6,4,\mathbb C),\qquad
\operatorname{EqSystemN}(6,4,W).
\]

## Logical outline

### 1. Contraction and local-axis forcing

From the official recursive perfect-matching sum, the proof derives the multilinear contraction identity for the four-color diagonal tensor. A Hall-type annihilator argument gives a pointwise axis witness. A finite-union-of-subspaces argument globalizes the witness, and vertex relabeling supplies the theorem at every vertex.

Every vertex therefore has four distinct incident axis witnesses, one per color.

### 2. Target matching selection

Each required monochromatic coefficient equals one, so at least one perfect-matching monomial is nonzero for each color. The proof selects one target perfect matching per color and proves compatibility with the local axis plans.

### 3. Target-orbit normalization

After color sorting and vertex permutation, every target quadruple lies in one of 21 canonical target orbits:

- 1 all-identical orbit;
- 6 locally infeasible orbits;
- 14 locally feasible nonidentical orbits.

Explicit certificates cover all 3,060 monotone target quadruples.

### 4. Finite witness-template classification

For the 14 feasible target orbits, all compatible local-plan choices are classified:

\[
830{,}909=830{,}880+29.
\]

- 830,880 choices contain a forbidden coloring with exactly one allowed, forced-nonzero matching monomial.
- 29 choices land in explicitly audited terminal support templates.

The finite result is represented by a 900-node certificate DAG. Lean checks the compact-code bridge, structural node semantics, unique-monomial certificates, and terminal-mask equality.

### 5. Terminal elimination

- 28 terminal masks are eliminated by explicit support-zero chains.
- One exceptional Cartesian mask is eliminated by two residual equations followed by a final unique nonzero monomial.
- The all-identical target orbit is eliminated by a 64-versus-36 coverage argument.
- The six locally infeasible target orbits have no compatible non-self local plan.

Every normalized target case is therefore contradictory.

## Important repaired defect

An earlier finite predicate omitted the real-neighbour condition

```lean
∀ c, p c ≠ v
```

although actual axis witnesses and both independent enumerators enforced it. A kernel-checked counterexample exposed the mismatch. The definition was repaired, every dependent certificate bridge was rechecked, and the final theorem was rebuilt after the repair.

## Limitations

This result closes exactly `(N,D) = (6,4)`. It does not by itself settle:

- `(8,3)` or any larger even vertex count;
- the unrestricted conjecture for all even `N ≥ 6` and `D ≥ 3`;
- the existence of a counterexample at a larger `N`.

The next theorem-development branch starts with color restriction and the `(8,3)` frontier.
